An independent project open to cooperation

GameSilkies is an independent experimental poultry crossbreeding and documentation project founded by José Flor – OzFlor in March 2024.

Although independently operated, the project is open to meaningful cooperation with individuals, researchers, poultry specialists, photographers, developers and organisations who respect its purpose and principles.

Cooperation can involve exchanging knowledge, technical assistance, scientific discussion, documentation improvements or the preservation of original material.

Areas of possible cooperation

AI-assisted documentation

Exploring tools and systems to organise, structure, retrieve and analyse daily poultry observations. Assistance with developing a free documentation workflow would be particularly welcome.

Original imagery and preservation

Selected original photographs and daily imagery may be supplied directly for preservation, documentation and appropriate research purposes, under agreed conditions.

Scientific and technical assistance

Exchange of expertise concerning poultry breeding, genetics, behaviour, natural development, husbandry and documentation methods.

Research and independent observation

Discussion, comparison of observations and collaborative work that may contribute to a better understanding of the birds and their development.

Documentation and image sharing

GameSilkies maintains different levels of documentation. Publicly released records are not equivalent to the complete private working archive.

Daily logs are published through the project's GitHub repository, while Odysee serves as the main public video archive.

Original photographs and associated image metadata may be provided directly to selected collaborators. This offers an opportunity to preserve original visual material in a suitable archive rather than relying exclusively on video-format publications.

Such sharing does not imply unrestricted access to private raw logs, unpublished observations or the entire project archive.

Original files remain attributed to their author. Image selection, transfer, storage, attribution and permitted use must be agreed in advance.

Project independence and private records

The original observations and private working records remain under the control of José Flor – OzFlor. Access to unpublished material is selective and subject to individual agreement.

Cooperation does not transfer authorship, ownership or unrestricted rights to use project material.

Conditions of cooperation

  • Cooperation must be mutually agreed.
  • The project is open to non-commercial cooperation only.
  • Original authorship and appropriate attribution must be respected.
  • Supplied images and documentation must not be commercially exploited.
  • The scope of access, permitted use, storage and redistribution must be agreed beforehand.
  • Cooperation must respect the independence and original purpose of GameSilkies.

No collaboration automatically grants permission for commercial use, republication or redistribution.

A personal philosophy

My work has always involved sharing knowledge and cooperating with others. I remain open to doing so through GameSilkies.

However, sharing should be based on respect, acknowledgement and responsibility.

I do not intend to turn this independent project into a commercial enterprise or surrender its original documentation to business interests.

I welcome genuine cooperation that helps preserve observations, improve knowledge and make useful contributions possible.

Interested in cooperating?

If you have relevant expertise, technical resources, research interests or an idea for cooperation, you are welcome to contact the project founder.

Please introduce yourself, explain your organisation or area of expertise, and describe what kind of cooperation you have in mind.
