Project Timeline

GameSilkies is an ongoing experiment. This timeline records significant stages in the development of the project, from its beginnings to its growing documentation and public presence.

This is a selected project chronology, not a complete daily log. Dates and milestones are included where they can be established from the project's records.

March 2024

Beginning of the GameSilkies Project

The independent experimental project begins, focusing on Gamefowl × Silkie crossbreeding and the observation of naturally produced hybrids.

The initial interest develops into a longer-term effort to document individual birds, their development and breeding possibilities.

2024–2025

Observation and experimental development

The project continues through individual bird observation, poultry husbandry, breeding experiences and the accumulation of records.

Documentation increasingly considers individual identity, development, behaviour, feeding, environmental conditions and breeding history.

2025

Development of recorded breeding lines

Individual birds and potential parental lines become part of the project's ongoing breeding documentation. Parentage and lineage are treated as matters to record and evaluate, not assume.

2026

Expansion of individual bird records

The project continues documenting individual hybrids, development, weights, behaviour and breeding-related observations.

Records increasingly distinguish direct observations from interpretations and unresolved questions.

2026

Structured digital documentation

Daily poultry records and project information are organized into a more structured digital archive. GitHub releases, Odysee publications and WordPress articles serve different documentation and publication purposes.

November 2026

Development of the GameSilkies website

GameSilkies.com becomes the project's dedicated website, bringing together its identity, breeding approach, documentation principles, publications and cooperation information.

An evolving history

The timeline will be expanded as additional milestones are documented and verified. It is not intended to replace the original daily records or individual bird histories.

The project preserves earlier observations even when later information changes their interpretation. Historical continuity is an essential part of the documentation.

The experiment is ongoing. No predetermined outcome or recognised breed status is implied by the existence of this timeline.
