Reading the records

Published material should be read with attention to its original context, date, individual bird identification and the distinction between direct observation and subsequent interpretation.

Records may be corrected or supplemented when new information becomes available. Later interpretations do not replace the historical fact that an earlier observation was made.

The project values continuity and traceability. A publication is a documented stage in an ongoing process, not necessarily a final scientific conclusion.
