GameSilkies

Documenting the Gamefowl × Silkie experiment — breeding, natural development, behaviour and individual birds.

About the project

GameSilkies is an independent experimental crossbreeding and documentation project founded by José Flor – OzFlor in March 2024. It records naturally produced Gamefowl × Silkie hybrids and follows their growth, behaviour, health observations, breeding decisions and subsequent generations.

The project brings together practical poultry keeping, long-term observation and systematic record-keeping. Its purpose is to document what happens over time, including uncertainty, variation and outcomes that may not fit initial expectations.

Project status

GameSilkies is a developing experimental project, not an established or recognised poultry breed. Observations, interpretations and hypotheses are kept distinct; conclusions may change as further evidence becomes available.

Documentation approach

Records aim to preserve chronology, original observations, source attribution and uncertainty. AI-assisted organisation may help structure or normalise material, but AI is not the observer, caretaker, breeder or originator of the records.
