About the project

GameSilkies is an independent experimental crossbreeding and documentation project founded by José Flor – OzFlor in March 2024.

It records naturally produced Gamefowl × Silkie hybrids and follows their growth, behaviour, health observations, breeding decisions and subsequent generations.

The project brings together practical poultry keeping, long-term observation and systematic record-keeping. Its purpose is to document what happens over time, including uncertainty, variation and unexpected outcomes.

Project status

GameSilkies is a developing experimental project, not an established or recognised poultry breed. Observations, interpretations and hypotheses are kept distinct. Conclusions may change as further evidence becomes available.

Project Origin & Independence Notice

While physically operated within Thailand as a matter of geography, the GameSilkies project is independent and is not a Thai project. Local conditions represent ongoing obstacles to its operation. The initiative belongs to José António Flor de Sousa (OzFlor) and is published under @Fazenda.Mondego.

Documentation approach

Records aim to preserve chronology, original observations, source attribution and uncertainty.

AI-assisted organisation and normalisation may help structure the material. However, AI is not the observer, caretaker, breeder or originator of the records. Original observations remain attributed to their author.
