1. Purpose and documentation principles

GameSilkies exists to document observations, experiences, successes, mistakes, corrections and lessons learned while maintaining a poultry flock and developing an independent experimental Gamefowl × Silkie crossbreeding project.

Observations may later prove incomplete or incorrect. Conclusions may change as new evidence becomes available. This is part of the public record and part of the purpose of maintaining chronological documentation.

GameSilkies is an experimental documentation project, not an established or officially recognised poultry breed.
