Origins

GameSilkies is an independent experimental crossbreeding and documentation initiative founded by José Flor – OzFlor in March 2024.

The project emerged from the keeping, observation and natural production of hybrids between Gamefowl and Silkie chickens.

What began as a practical breeding experiment developed into a longer-term documentation effort, following individual birds, their development, behaviour, characteristics and subsequent generations.

The project is physically operated in Thailand, but its identity and scope are independent of national or institutional affiliation.

Purpose

The central purpose of GameSilkies is to document an experimental crossbreeding process over time.

Rather than concentrating exclusively on desirable outcomes, the project records development as it actually occurs, including unexpected results, individual variation, difficulties and uncertainty.

Its documentation includes:

  • Individual bird identification and histories.
  • Parentage, breeding decisions and generations.
  • Eggs, hatching and natural development.
  • Growth, body weight and physical characteristics.
  • Behaviour and interactions.
  • Health and husbandry observations.
  • Environmental conditions and practical experiences.
  • Photographic and video documentation.
Experimental status

GameSilkies is not an established, officially recognised or standardised poultry breed. It is an ongoing experimental crossbreeding project.

The birds and their characteristics are documented as they develop. No predetermined breed standard or guaranteed outcome is claimed.

Independence

GameSilkies is independently conceived, maintained and documented by its founder.

It is not presented as an institutional research programme, university project or officially recognised breeding organisation.

The work combines practical poultry keeping, individual observation, experimentation and systematic record-keeping.

Cooperation with other individuals or organisations is welcome when based on mutual agreement and respect for the project's independence and non-commercial principles.

Documentation philosophy

The value of a record is not limited to whether an interpretation eventually proves correct. Its original observation, chronology and context remain important.

Observation before interpretation

Original observations should remain distinguishable from subsequent explanations or hypotheses.

Preserve uncertainty

Unknown, incomplete or uncertain information should not be replaced with invented certainty.

Chronological continuity

Records follow the actual sequence of events, rather than forcing observations into artificial daily boundaries.

Individual identity

Each bird has its own history. Individual records should not be confused or unnecessarily merged.

Interpretations may evolve

Later evidence may change an interpretation, but it should not rewrite the original observation.

AI as an assisting tool

AI may assist with organisation, normalisation and analysis, but it is not the observer, caretaker, breeder or originator of the records.

Records and archives

The project maintains different publication channels for different types of material.

  • Odysee: Main public archive for video documentation and project publications.
  • GitHub: Daily poultry log releases and structured digital records.
  • WordPress: Articles, explanations and documentation standards.
  • GameSilkies.com: Main website and permanent project entry point.

Public releases represent selected documentation. They do not imply that every original observation, private raw log or unpublished record is publicly available.

Project founder

José António Flor de Sousa, known as José Flor – OzFlor, is an independent naturalist poultry farmer, breeder and observer.

His work focuses on poultry breeding, behaviour, natural development, husbandry and the systematic documentation of individual birds and breeding populations.

GameSilkies reflects a practical, long-term commitment to observing and recording what happens rather than presenting an idealised version of the experiment.

Project principles

GameSilkies is built around continuity, independent observation, responsible documentation and respect for the original work.

The project does not claim that every question has an answer. It aims to preserve enough information for observations to remain useful, revisitable and open to future interpretation.
