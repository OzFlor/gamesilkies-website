Cooperation enquiries

GameSilkies is open to appropriate non-commercial scientific, technical and documentation cooperation.

Cooperation is based on mutual agreement. The sharing of selected records, original imagery or other project material is considered individually. Private raw logs are not automatically available for unrestricted access or redistribution.

For further information, please read the Cooperation page.

Before contacting

  • Identify the subject of your enquiry clearly.
  • Include relevant project or publication references where possible.
  • Do not post private or sensitive information in public comments.
  • Allow time for a response; this is an independently operated project.
Contact through an external platform is subject to that platform's own availability, account settings and communication policies. No response time or communication channel is guaranteed.
